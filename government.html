UNDERSTAND YOUR GOVERNMENT

How Does Local Government Work?

Government can feel complicated. LokLink explains it in simple language so citizens know who is responsible for what.

FROM YOUR LOCALITY TO THE GOVERNMENT

Your Voice Can Travel Through Multiple Levels

When you report a local issue, it may be handled at different administrative levels depending on the type and seriousness of the problem.

01
🏘️
LOCAL LEVEL

Village / Local Body

This is the level closest to citizens. Local issues such as sanitation, street maintenance and community facilities may be handled here.

Common responsibilities
  • Local roads
  • Cleanliness
  • Street lights
  • Water-related local issues
↓
02
🏢
BLOCK LEVEL

Block Administration

Block-level administration coordinates development activities across several villages and local areas.

Common responsibilities
  • Development programs
  • Rural services
  • Scheme implementation
  • Local development coordination
↓
03
🏛️
DISTRICT LEVEL

District Administration

District administration coordinates government departments and development activities across the district.

Common responsibilities
  • District development
  • Department coordination
  • Public services
  • Implementation monitoring
↓
04
🏛️
STATE LEVEL

State Government

State government manages departments, policies and many public services across the state.

Common responsibilities
  • State policies
  • Major departments
  • State schemes
  • Public administration
↓
05
🇮🇳
NATIONAL LEVEL

Central Government

The Government of India manages national policies, central departments and nationwide programs.

Common responsibilities
  • National policies
  • Central schemes
  • National programs
  • Central departments
