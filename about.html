ABOUT LOKLINK

Making Government Easier to Understand.

LokLink is a citizen-focused platform designed to connect people with information about their locality, government services, complaints and welfare schemes.

🔗

Locality + Government

OUR MISSION

Connecting Citizens With Their Government

Many people know there is a government system around them, but they may not know exactly who is responsible for a particular issue.

LokLink aims to make this information easier to discover by bringing locality information, complaint reporting, government schemes and administrative information into one platform.
